About Us

Commercial and Residential Hygiene solutions We're a professional cleaning company dedicated to making homes, offices, and commercial spaces cleaner, healthier, and more comfortable. Our team delivers reliable, detail-focused cleaning services tailored to the unique needs of every client. From routine home cleaning to deep cleaning and commercial maintenance, we use proven cleaning methods, quality products, and careful attention to detail to deliver results you can see and feel. We believe a clean space is more than just a good first impression — it creates a healthier environment, saves you time, and gives you peace of mind. That's why we show up on time, work professionally, and treat every space with the care it deserves. Our goal is simple: leave every space spotless, every time.

Our Cleaning Services

Furniture cleaning

We provide professional deep cleaning and detailed care for all types of furniture, helping restore its freshness, appearance, and comfort. Our skilled and well-equipped team uses carefully selected cleaning products and effective techniques to tackle dirt, stubborn stains, and unpleasant odours. From sofas and armchairs to other upholstered furnishings, we pay close attention to every detail, leaving your furniture cleaner, fresher, and looking its best. At Gorgons Cleaning Company, we give your furniture the care it deserves.

Cars/Automotive Detailing

Give your vehicle the care and attention it deserves with our professional automotive cleaning and detailing services. We go beyond ordinary washing to restore your vehicle's shine, enhance its appearance, and leave its interior clean, fresh, and inviting. Using carefully selected cleaning products, quality fragrances, and effective odour eliminators, we ensure your vehicle looks immaculate, feels refreshed, and smells great. At Gorgons Cleaning Company, we help you enjoy a spotless, well-maintained vehiclethat you can take pride in.

Home cleaning

A clean home is a happy home. We provide comprehensive residential cleaning services, including general living-area cleaning, bedroom cleaning, kitchen and bathroom sanitization, furniture and upholstery cleaning, surface disinfection, and deep cleaning. Our goal is to transform your home into a spotless, fresh, hygienic, and welcoming environment where you can relax, feel comfortable, and enjoy the peace of mind you deserve.

Corporate areas/Office cleanings

We provide reliable and thorough cleaning services for offices,corporate buildings, and commercial workspaces. Our services include desk and workstation cleaning, floor care, glass and window cleaning, waste disposal, restroom sanitization, and high-touch surface disinfection. We help maintain a clean, hygienic, and organized working environment that promotes employee well-being, productivity, and a professional image for your business.
